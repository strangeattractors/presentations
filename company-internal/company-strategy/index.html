Three convictions

Why this bet, why now.

1

Personal AI lacks context, workflow, memory.

Tired of copy-pasting between Email, Calendar, Drive and ChatGPT? We're giving users one AI inbox for all communication — and on-the-job learning from managerial voice feedback, the way humans learn.

2

AGI can be democratized — without prompt engineering.

Everyone can talk about what they want from their AI. We convert that into reliable custom prompts that self-evaluate and self-improve. YouTube let anyone run a channel; we envision a world where anyone can run their own AI, teaching it their skills, to work 10 jobs for them.

3

GenAI can reduce medical mistakes — and save lives.

Healthcare is understaffed: patients lack rapid affordable access, clinicians are burned out. Imagine if patients could talk to their doctor's AI.

Alignment Q&A

The questions the team and investors keep asking. Draft, open for debate.

What do we do? What's our focus?
Our AIs give people instant 1-shot access to services that currently take hours or days to find. Book in one go: an optometrist, a contract lawyer, a boom lift, a ride. This helps professionals book more qualified leads and fill their capacity.
Should we focus exclusively on healthcare?
Healthcare is our favorite vertical — most meaningful impact — but it's not our only vertical. Too risky, slow-moving, AI-cautious. And needlessly limiting: healthcare features carry over to other verticals. Being horizontal means we can be viral.
Should we focus exclusively on solopreneurs?
No. The lack of team potential makes the unit economics unattractive. Our LTV:CAC sweet spot is 20–1,000 person companies. Also: solopreneur-only would bar us from >99% of healthcare. Ever consulted a doctor who had zero staff?
What's our vision?
AI agent creator economy: top users specialize their AI characters to their profession and distribute them to others in their field. Already happening: Jamil → Amanda → oncology; Enrique → Thomas → equipment rental; Carlos → Sarah → transportation.
If we're vertical- and size-agnostic, will we lose to specialized competitors?
We prioritize vertical software integrations in our top 3 verticals: Construction, Healthcare, Real Estate. Mid-term we cover many verticals by letting our AI-creator users do the specialization & distribution via user-generated industry templates — they have more domain expertise and connections than we do.
What do we not do?
We are not offering the product outside the US (until we reach US commercial leadership).
We are not building an Android version (until we expand to an Android-dominant geo).
We are not offering medical-advice AI — we help healthcare professionals book qualified patients & fill available capacity.
Isn't viral consumer tech more exciting than B2B CRM software?
We're a hybrid. Consumer side: humanized AIs with empathy, social sharing loops, gamified AI specialization & distribution, YouSquared best-practice news feed & forum. B2B side: CRM lead capture, tracking, 3rd-party integrations.
Are we building a browser version?
Yes. Every popular app has one — typing on a desktop keyboard is more productive. AI medical scribes are predominantly desktop. Even Breezy (solopreneur-focused) offers a browser version.
What do we expand to next?
Calls → Calendar → Texts → Email → Note-taking → Web Chat. Some people prefer to call, others text, email, chat. Email will be game-changing: 70–90% of leads in almost every vertical (incl. healthcare). We already lose healthcare leads to Breezy, who cover all communication channels with a similar-sized dev team.
How does the Verizon partnership fit in?
The most strategic possible partnership for us. ⅓ of all voicemails in America are triggered by Verizon. Verizon is best positioned to upgrade the American phone-call economy — defaulting to voice AI rather than voicemail when a human is unavailable. They can distribute and integrate us so businesses and phone-driven professionals can activate YouSquared easily.
When do we come out of stealth?
March–May 2026, with Verizon.
