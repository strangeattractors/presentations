What this tells us
  • Mar rebound driven by ad-spend cuts — Google spend dropped £18K → £6K while onboarded held at 121. CAC fell from $227 → $70.
  • Activation still the biggest CAC lever — 33% vs 70% target. Every 10pp gain cuts CAC ~20%.
