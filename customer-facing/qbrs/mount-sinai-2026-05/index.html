The Hidden Insight

Half of Dr. I.A.'s tasks belong to someone else.

The AI didn't just take messages. It generated 51 action items from those 148 calls. We categorized every one. 26 of them (51%) should be assigned to a teammate — an NP, a medical secretary, a social worker. Today, Dr. I.A. has to triage all 51 himself.

04
The Build

One tap. Right teammate. Right context. Right now.

We're shipping a Team feature that lets Dr. I.A. assign any AI-generated task to a colleague with one tap. The teammate receives the task with the AI's full call context — caller name, callback number, what they need, the suggested follow-up text. No re-explaining. No phone tag. No dropped patient detail.

Why this is the right next build for Dr. I.A.

  • ~26 of his 51 tasks/month shouldn't live with him at all.
  • Every category has an obvious owner already on his team — NPs ("Ruby", "Javel" appear repeatedly in his calls), secretaries, social workers.
  • The AI already captured who, why, when, what's needed, and a draft message — the assign step is trivial UX.
  • Dr. I.A. has authority over these roles: patient outcomes improve when the right person acts within minutes, not hours.

What the patient experiences

  • Faster home-care letters — secretary acts immediately, not next time Dr. I.A. clears his inbox.
  • Faster medication administration — NP places routine orders within minutes, not hours.
  • Smoother discharges — social worker has the family contact and context already.
