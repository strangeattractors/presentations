What happens behind the scenes

  • AI identifies eligible patients from Ahava’s records — BMI > 25, no active GLP-1 prescription, provider opted in.
  • Personalized text sent from Ahava’s dedicated line — patients see their medical center, not a third party.
  • AI handles replies in real time — answers questions, confirms interest, captures preferred times.
  • Consultation booked — interested patients are connected to their provider or the scheduling team.
  • After hours covered — 45% of replies come outside business hours. The AI never sleeps.

Next Step

Every provider chooses to participate.

Ahava has ~100 providers across six locations. Each one has a different patient panel, a different relationship with their patients, and different comfort levels with outreach. The next step is simple: we reach out to each provider individually, explain what we’re doing, and let them opt in.
👥
Provider briefing
Short 1-on-1 with each doctor to explain the program, show the patient text flow, and answer questions.
✅
Opt-in confirmation
Each provider confirms which patient segments they want included. Full control, no surprises.
📈
Per-provider reporting
Every doctor sees their own outreach stats, patient replies, and booked consultations in the dashboard.

Why provider-by-provider

  • Trust. Patients respond because they see their doctor’s name, not a generic center message. That trust requires the provider’s buy-in.
  • Relevance. A cardiologist’s BMI-eligible patients are different from a dermatologist’s. The AI tailors outreach by specialty.
  • Control. Providers who aren’t ready can wait. No pressure, no friction. They join when they see colleagues succeeding.
The math, per provider
Average panel: ~900 patients
BMI > 25: ~42% → ~378 eligible
At 50/day pace: full panel reached in ~8 days
At 10% conversion: ~38 consultations per provider
Ongoing: new patients + re-engagement cycles
