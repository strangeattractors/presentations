Roadmap for Hair Shunnarah

Where Hank goes next — and what it unlocks.

1
Hank is best-in-class voice AI.
Sub-second latency, natural turn-taking, empathic prosody — callers routinely don't realize they're talking to AI.
Everything else in this roadmap rides on how human the voice feels. Smooth speech is the foundation.
Target
< 800ms P50 response latency · graceful barge-in · zero same-day regressions. Today Hank beats the latency target on most calls; improvements ship weekly.
  • Sub-second response latency — P50 < 800ms from end-of-caller-speech to Hank's first spoken token. The single biggest determinant of "feels human" vs "feels like a bot."
  • Natural turn detection — don't cut callers off mid-thought; accommodate long pauses for distressed or elderly speakers (LT's 12-minute Saturday call is exhibit A)
  • Graceful barge-in — if the caller interrupts, Hank stops cleanly and listens; no talk-over, no restart
  • Empathic prosody — intonation, emphasis, and pacing match a human intake specialist on their best day, not monotone TTS
  • Accent & dialect handling — Southern LA speech patterns, "y'all", Cajun cadence, hands-free car audio, background noise
  • Continuous quality monitoring — every call scored for smoothness + caller-satisfaction signals; regressions flagged same-day and rolled back
2
Hank qualifies, signs up & transfers new clients.
First in line on every new caller, 24/7. Contract signed on the call; warm hand-off to your intake team.
Signed contracts on the first call — no week-later follow-up, no lost momentum.
Pace check
At the current rate Hank hands over ~3 qualified new-client leads per month — 10 in about 3–4 months. In hurricane season (Jul–Nov) that rate nearly triples: 6–7 / month, so 10 in about 6 weeks. Finishing this checklist is what puts those leads directly into signed retainers instead of voicemail hand-offs.
  • Migrate HSTA to the YouSquared Team plan
  • Migrate Hank to the dynamic prompt — verify the migration is successful (e.g. do call-transfer tool calls trigger appropriately? does Hank still qualify the right leads? does contract-send fire in the right context?)
  • Reinstate signing tool
  • Add PI contract to signing tool
  • Ensure Hank sends the correct contract in the correct situation
