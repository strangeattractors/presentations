01 — The problem

Cancer patients can't reach their doctor when it matters most

A single physician manages 20–40 active patients undergoing treatment. Each one generates family calls, symptom questions, lab result inquiries, and after-hours emergencies. The EHR inbox alone can hold 1,000+ unread messages. Missed calls don't just frustrate — they delay triage.

What your AI secretary does

Every unanswered call is a patient who feels abandoned. YouSquared answers instantly — triaging, informing, connecting — without replacing the physician, by freeing them. Works alongside your EHR — we handle the voice channels EHRs can't: after-hours inbound, outbound voice outreach, procedure intake.
