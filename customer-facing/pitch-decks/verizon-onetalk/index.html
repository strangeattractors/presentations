02 — Setup

Live on One Talk in one day

YouSquared sits in front of One Talk. Nothing downstream changes — every user, hunt group and voicemail box stays exactly where it is.

One change to make
Repoint your main number at the YouSquared endpoint. That's it.
No carrier work
No Verizon provisioning, no SIP changes, no rebuild.
5-minute rollback
If anything goes sideways, repoint back to the original auto attendant.
From yes to live in ~1 day · including a 30-minute discovery call & a 15-minute cutover.
In the One Talk admin: Call Forwarding → Always → forward to your YouSquared number. That's the entire technical change.
