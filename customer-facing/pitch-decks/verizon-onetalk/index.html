Handles without a transfer Self-serve

If the answer is the same for every caller and doesn't change hour to hour, the AI handles it.

  • Hours, location, directions, parking
  • FAQs from website or knowledge base
  • Status questions ("Are you open Sunday?")
  • Simple scheduling and intake capture
  • Message-taking with structured transcripts to email/CRM

Transfers to a human Warm handoff

If the answer depends on who's calling or what's happening right now, it's a transfer — into the existing One Talk destinations.

  • Anything the AI isn't confident about
  • "Let me talk to someone" — instant route
  • Emergencies and urgency keywords
  • Sales, billing, support — routed by intent
  • After-hours escalations to on-call staff

The 30-minute discovery sprint

One screen-share. Six questions. Everything YouSquared needs to go live. For an existing One Talk customer you don't need a CSR or a bill — you need the answers below. Use this as your call agenda.

0–5 min
Inbound number map
Which number(s) will the AI answer? Usually just the main published number — but confirm: "If I call the number on your website right now, what happens?"
5–15 min
Transfer destination map
Every existing hunt group, user, voicemail box, and after-hours path the AI might transfer to. Use the customer's real internal names.
15–22 min
Self-serve intent list
The top 5–10 reasons people call that the AI can resolve without a human. Almost always #1 is hours and location.
22–27 min
Escalation rules
Emergency keywords ("leak", "down", "bleeding"), after-hours destinations, VIP handling, fallback if everyone's busy.
27–30 min
Knowledge sources
Where the AI pulls answers from — public website, Google Business Profile, FAQ PDF, services sheet, or a 20-minute Q&A doc you'll give them a template for.
Partner tip

Walk through in this order every time. The intent list is easier to discuss after they've mapped their transfer destinations — it forces them to think about what should NOT be transferred.

05 Technical cutover

It's just a number repoint

There's exactly one technical change required to go live: the inbound number that currently hits a One Talk auto attendant or hunt group now hits the YouSquared endpoint. Everything downstream is untouched.

Direct number routing Recommended · Model 1

The customer's main number points at a YouSquared-provided number/SIP endpoint. The AI answers, does its work, and when it needs to transfer, it dials the existing One Talk hunt group or user DID like any other outside caller. Cleanest setup, easiest rollback, no carrier-side changes. (Model 2 — full SIP integration with native caller ID — is the upgrade path after 30–60 days of use.)

07 Objection handling

What customers ask, and what to say

A quick reference for the VAR. Customers ask these almost every time — answer them in the same words and the call moves forward.

"Will this replace my phone system?"
No. YouSquared sits in front of the existing One Talk setup. Every user, hunt group, voicemail box stays exactly where it is. The AI just answers the main number first.
"What if the AI gets something wrong?"
It's configured to transfer when it's not confident. If the caller ever says "let me talk to a person", it transfers immediately. And the whole thing rolls back to the original configuration in under five minutes.
"Will my customers know they're talking to an AI?"
That's a choice. The agent can be explicitly disclosed ("I'm the virtual assistant for Acme") or introduced more neutrally. Most customers start with explicit disclosure — it's the right call ethically, and callers are far more tolerant when they know what it is.
"Can the AI call me on my cell when needed?"
Yes. The AI can transfer to any number — a One Talk user, a hunt group, a mobile, a third-party after-hours service. Transfer destinations are just phone numbers.
"What if we already have an auto attendant we like?"
YouSquared is almost always a better experience. But if the customer is attached to it, deploy the AI in front of the auto attendant — the AI handles what it can and transfers anything else into the existing menu.
"How long does this take to set up?"
For an existing One Talk customer with a good knowledge source: one discovery call, two to three days of configuration, a test call, and a 15-minute cutover. Most accounts go live within a week of saying yes.
