01 — The problem

You carry the risk. You don't have the patients on the phone.

IPAs are accountable for outcomes, Star ratings, and total cost of care across hundreds of independent practices — but the patient still belongs to the doctor, not to you. The work is patient-side: adherence, AWVs, transitions of care, gap closure. The cost is RN-shaped: $5–15 PMPM spent on care coordination today, with most outbound calls going to voicemail.

02 — The solution

One patient-engagement layer across your network

YouSquared answers, calls, schedules, and triages on your behalf — voice + text, 24/7, multilingual. Plugs into each member practice via HL7 FHIR, hands the work back to your nurses and care managers structured and prioritized.

Inbound

The IPA's 24/7 patient line

  • Triage & nurse-advice
  • Post-visit Q&A
  • Refills & medication questions
  • Multilingual (11+ languages)
  • Escalation to RN / care manager
Outbound

Care-management at scale

  • Adherence reminders (PDC measures)
  • AWV / preventive scheduling
  • Post-discharge TRC follow-up
  • Gap closure (mammo, A1c, colo)
  • CG-CAHPS / experience surveys
